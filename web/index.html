Help & Support Center

Employee Onboarding & Management

Q: What is the standard procedure for onboarding a new hire?

A: Navigate to the Employees module and select 'Add New Employee'. Ensure you have all mandatory legal documents (ID, Tax Forms) before saving the profile. The system will automatically generate a unique Employee ID.

Q: How do I change an employee's employment status (e.g., from Full-time to Freelance)?

A: Locate the employee in the directory, click 'Edit', and update the 'Employment Type' field. Note: Changing this status may affect their benefits and payroll calculations.

Leave & Payroll Processing

Q: Does approved leave automatically sync with the monthly payroll?

A: Yes. Once a supervisor approves a time-off request in the Leave Management section, any unpaid leave will automatically trigger a deduction in the corresponding month's Payrolls run.

Q: How do I generate the final payslips for the current billing cycle?

A: Go to Payrolls, review the calculated amounts for discrepancies. Once verified, use the 'Export Data' function at the top of the screen to generate a CSV report for your accounting department or bank transfer.

Data Security & Backup

Q: How secure is the employee data stored in this system?

A: This is a standalone HRM system. All sensitive data (Salaries, Performance reviews) is stored locally via an encrypted SQLite database on this specific machine. No data is transmitted to external cloud servers.

Q: How often should I backup the system?

A: We recommend performing a manual backup weekly or immediately after processing monthly payrolls. Go to Settings > 'System Backup' to generate a secure local copy.
